# TAKAI BRAND CORE

## Who We Are

TAKAI Group is a Ruiru/Nairobi-based private transport company providing professional 14-seater transport with a driver.

## What We Do

- Airport transfers
- Corporate transport
- Group movements
- Private travel
- Tour operator support
- Overflow transport
- Pre-booked multi-day private hire

## What We Don't Do

TAKAI is a transport company.

We do not operate safaris, game drives or park activities.

We move people reliably to where they need to go.

## Who We Serve

- Hotels
- Airbnb operators
- Corporates
- Events
- Groups
- Private clients
