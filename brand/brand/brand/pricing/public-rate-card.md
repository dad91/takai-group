# TAKAI PUBLIC RATE CARD

## Base

TAKAI Group operates from Ruiru/Nairobi.

Our public private-hire pricing is based on:

- Vehicle time
- Vehicle distance
- Driver time
- Operating costs
- The commitment required from the vehicle

Passenger count is not the primary pricing variable.

## Standalone / Private-Hire Day

| Duration | Distance | Price |
|---|---:|---:|
| Local protection | 0–60 km | KES 10,000 |
| 3 hours | Up to 80 km | KES 12,000 |
| 5 hours | Up to 80 km | KES 12,500 |
| 8 hours | Up to 80 km | KES 14,000 |
| 11 hours | Up to 80 km | KES 15,000 |

### Distance Rule

For bookings exceeding 80 km:

**Additional distance = KES 65 per km**

Total kilometres are based on the vehicle's operational movement from the Ruiru base.

## Important

The public rate card is for standalone/private-hire work.

When a client is committing the vehicle for multiple days, overnight work or a longer movement, use the **Universal Commitment Calculator** instead.

## Minimum Charge

Minimum standalone booking:

**KES 10,000**

## Pricing Principle

TAKAI prices the work required from the vehicle, not simply the number of passengers.

The objective is to protect:

- Operating costs
- Driver costs
- Vehicle wear
- Time
- Opportunity cost
- Business margin

## Quote Rule

Never invent a destination-specific price simply because a destination is popular.

Use the correct pricing system for the type of booking.

## Status

This is the current public pricing structure.

Changes to pricing should be documented and committed to GitHub so the business maintains a clear history of pricing decisions.
