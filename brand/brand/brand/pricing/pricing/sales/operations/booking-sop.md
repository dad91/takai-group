# TAKAI BOOKING SOP

## Purpose

Every confirmed booking should follow the same basic process.

The goal is:

- Clear information
- No avoidable surprises
- Professional communication
- Reliable vehicle preparation
- Proper follow-through

---

## 1. Receive the Request

Collect:

- Client name
- Company name, if applicable
- Contact number
- Pickup location
- Destination
- Date
- Pickup time
- Estimated duration
- Estimated distance
- Trip type
- Passenger requirements
- Special instructions

---

## 2. Confirm the Price

Use the correct TAKAI pricing system.

### Standalone / Private Hire

Use:

`pricing/public-rate-card.md`

### Vehicle Commitment

Use:

`pricing/commitment-calculator.md`

Never guess a price.

---

## 3. Confirm Availability

Before confirming the booking:

- Confirm vehicle availability.
- Confirm driver availability.
- Confirm the required time.
- Confirm any overnight requirements.
- Confirm any unusual route or operating requirements.

A quote is not a confirmed booking.

---

## 4. Send the Quote

The client should clearly understand:

- What is included
- Price
- Date
- Time
- Pickup
- Destination
- Vehicle type
- Driver
- Additional charges, if applicable
- Payment terms
- Cancellation terms

Avoid hidden costs.

---

## 5. Booking Confirmation

A booking becomes confirmed only after the required confirmation/payment conditions have been met.

Record the booking.

---

## 6. Before the Trip

Confirm:

- Vehicle condition
- Fuel
- Cleanliness
- Tyres
- Lights
- Required documents
- Driver readiness
- Pickup location
- Client contact
- Route
- Special instructions

---

## 7. Driver Standard

The driver must:

- Arrive prepared
- Be clean and professionally presented
- Communicate respectfully
- Drive responsibly
- Avoid unnecessary arguments
- Never ask clients for tips
- Never create confusion about payment
- Report problems early

The driver represents TAKAI.

---

## 8. During the Trip

Priorities:

**Safety → Punctuality → Communication → Client experience**

If a delay or problem occurs:

1. Identify the problem.
2. Inform the client as early as possible.
3. Communicate the expected impact.
4. Escalate internally when necessary.
5. Document significant incidents.

Never hide a transport problem.

---

## 9. After the Trip

Confirm:

- Trip completed
- Final kilometres where required
- Additional charges, if applicable
- Payment status
- Vehicle condition
- Any client feedback
- Any operational issue

---

## 10. Learn From Every Trip

A completed booking should provide information.

Ask:

- What went well?
- What went wrong?
- What could be faster?
- What could be clearer?
- What should TAKAI change?

Useful lessons should become improvements to the system.

---

## Core Principle

TAKAI should not depend on one person's memory.

If a process happens repeatedly, document it.

If a mistake happens repeatedly, fix the system.

If a better way is discovered, update the system.
