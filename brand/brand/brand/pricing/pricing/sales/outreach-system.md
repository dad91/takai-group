# TAKAI B2B OUTREACH SYSTEM

## Objective

Build a predictable pipeline of qualified B2B transport opportunities.

Primary target:

**10 new qualified B2B prospects per day.**

The goal is not to send 10 random messages.

The goal is to identify businesses with a real reason to need TAKAI.

---

## Target Buyers

### 1. Tour Operators & DMCs

Look for companies handling:

- Airport arrivals
- Hotel movements
- Guest transfers
- Group movements
- Upcountry transfers
- Multi-day private transport
- Overflow transport

### 2. Hotels & Airbnb Operators

Look for businesses that may need:

- Airport transfers
- Guest transport
- Group movements
- Corporate transport
- Event movements

### 3. Corporate Clients

Look for:

- Staff movements
- Airport transfers
- Events
- Conferences
- Delegations
- Executive group transport

### 4. Events

Look for:

- Conferences
- Weddings
- Corporate events
- Exhibitions
- Group events

---

## Ideal Prospect

A strong prospect has:

1. A clear need for transport.
2. Regular or high-value clients.
3. A reputation to protect.
4. Evidence that transport coordination matters.
5. A decision-maker we can identify.

---

## The TAKAI Angle

We are not selling a van.

We are selling:

**Transport certainty.**

The core B2B message:

> PRIVATE TRANSPORT FOR OPERATORS WHO CAN'T AFFORD TRANSPORT PROBLEMS.

---

## Observable Evidence

Before contacting a prospect, look for evidence such as:

- Airport arrival services
- Guest transfers
- Group tours
- Hotel partnerships
- Safari itineraries
- Corporate travel
- Event transport
- Existing transport arrangements
- Frequent guest movements

Do not invent a problem.

Use observable evidence.

---

## First Contact

The first message should be:

- Short
- Relevant
- Human
- Specific to the prospect
- Easy to respond to

Do not send a generic company biography.

Lead with why TAKAI may be useful to them.

## Follow-Up

A prospect is not considered lost because they did not answer the first message.

Use a simple follow-up sequence.

### Follow-Up 1

Brief reminder with a useful reason to continue the conversation.

### Follow-Up 2

Clarify the specific transport support TAKAI can provide.

### Final Follow-Up

Close the loop professionally and leave the door open.

Never harass a prospect.

## TAKAI Outreach Test

Before sending:

**Why this company?**

**Why now?**

**What evidence do we have?**

**Why would TAKAI be useful to them?**

**Is this message specific enough to feel human?**

If these questions cannot be answered, improve the prospect research before sending.
