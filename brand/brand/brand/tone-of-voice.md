# TAKAI TONE OF VOICE

## How TAKAI Sounds

TAKAI sounds confident, clear, direct and human.

We are professional without sounding corporate or boring.

We are Nairobi-real.

We do not sound like a marketing agency.

We do not sound desperate for bookings.

We do not exaggerate.

We do not make promises we cannot keep.

## Our Voice

### Clear

Say what we mean.

Avoid complicated language.

### Confident

We know what we provide.

We don't need to shout.

### Human

Talk like a real person.

Professional does not mean robotic.

### Direct

Get to the point.

Respect the customer's time.

### Honest

If something is outside our service, say so.

If a vehicle, route or booking needs confirmation, say so.

## TAKAI Writing Rules

Use short sentences.

Use active language.

Lead with the customer's problem or need.

Explain the solution simply.

Use specific information whenever possible.

Avoid unnecessary emojis.

Avoid corporate buzzwords.

Avoid fake urgency.

Avoid exaggerated claims.

## The Test

Before publishing anything, ask:

Would a serious Nairobi business owner believe this?

Would a tour operator trust us with their client?

Does this sound like TAKAI?
